## 🧩 About

**Starfield Overlay** is a comprehensive mod menu for Starfield featuring an in-game overlay menu, Lua script execution, player and ship stat editors, inventory manager, and teleportation tools.

Based on popular mods like **SFSE**, **Baka Achievement Enabler**, and **Starfield Script Extender**.
